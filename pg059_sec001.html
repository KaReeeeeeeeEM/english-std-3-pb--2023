Unit Seven

Reading a variety of texts

Introduction

In English, words are pronounced as a combination of sounds. The combination of sounds pronounced as a single unit is called a syllable. In this unit, you will read words and identify their syllables. The competencies developed will enable you to hear, pronounce and place word stress and intonation appropriately.
