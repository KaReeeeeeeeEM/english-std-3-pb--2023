Introduction

The English Pupil’s Book Standard Three has been prepared in accordance with the 2023 English Language Syllabus for Primary Education, Standards III–VI, issued by the Ministry of Education, Science and Technology. This textbook is the Third Edition, improved from the Second Edition of 2021 of the English Pupil’s Book Standard Three, published by TIE in accordance with the 2016 Basic Education Syllabus for Standard III.

The book is aimed at enabling the pupil to develop competencies in listening, speaking, reading and writing in English. It has eight units. Each unit contains oral and writing activities that are systematically organised to enable the pupil to practise and acquire the intended competencies.

Additional learning resources are available in the TIE e-Library at https://ol.tie.go.tz or ol.tie.go.tz
