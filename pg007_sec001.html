Unit One

Listening and speaking

Introduction

Listening and speaking skills are important for effective communication. In this unit, you will learn to listen to and communicate information orally in English. The competencies developed will enable you to communicate with friends, teachers, parents and other people in different situations.

Activity 1: Listening and writing

(a) Listen to the words read to you and write them.

(b) Listen to the sentences read to you and write them.
