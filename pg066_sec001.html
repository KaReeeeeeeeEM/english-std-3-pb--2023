Unit Eight

Recognising the basic features of spoken English

Introduction

Spoken English has certain basic features that differentiate it from other forms of delivery.In this unit, you will practise speaking English and recognise the differences in pronunciation between words.You will also recognise the differences in intonation between sentences.The competencies developed will enable you to pronounce words correctly and vary stress and intonation between sentences.
